# University Financial Aid Office: Merit Scholarship & Retention Guidelines

## Section 1: Institutional Honors & Merit Scholarship Retention
- **Presidential Honors Merit Scholarship:**
  - Annual Award: Up to $15,000 per academic year.
  - Enrollment Requirement: Continuous full-time enrollment (minimum 12 credit hours per semester).
  - GPA Retention Requirement: Must achieve and maintain a cumulative GPA of at least 3.25, evaluated at the conclusion of each spring semester.
- **Dean's Academic Excellence Scholarship:**
  - Annual Award: Up to $8,000 per academic year.
  - GPA Retention Requirement: Cumulative GPA of at least 3.00.

## Section 2: Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP) for Federal Aid
- Minimum Cumulative GPA: 2.00 after completing two academic semesters.
- Completion Pace: Students must successfully complete at least 67% of all attempted cumulative credit hours.
- Maximum Timeframe: Aid eligibility ceases once a student has attempted 150% of the published credit hours required for their degree program (e.g., 180 attempted credit hours for a 120-credit degree).
