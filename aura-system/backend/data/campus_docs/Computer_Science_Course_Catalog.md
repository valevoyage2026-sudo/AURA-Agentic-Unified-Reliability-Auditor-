# Department of Computer Science: Undergraduate Course Catalog & Prerequisite Map

## Lower-Division Requirements
- **CS 101: Introduction to Computer Science (4 Credits)**
  - Description: Fundamentals of structured programming, algorithmic decomposition, and basic data types.
  - Prerequisites: High School Algebra or MATH 101.
  - Corequisites: CS 101L (Programming Lab, 0 credits) and MATH 115 (Calculus I, 4 credits).

- **CS 201: Data Structures & Algorithms (4 Credits)**
  - Description: Stacks, queues, linked lists, trees, graphs, sorting, and asymptotic Big-O runtime analysis.
  - Prerequisites: CS 101 with a minimum grade of 'C' (2.00) and MATH 115 with a minimum grade of 'C'.
  - Corequisites: None.

## Upper-Division Requirements
- **CS 301: Systems Programming & Operating Systems (4 Credits)**
  - Description: Memory management, Unix shell programming, processes, threads, concurrency, and virtual memory.
  - Prerequisites: CS 201 with a minimum grade of 'C'.
  - Special Regulation: CS 301 does NOT require Math 400 or Advanced Linear Algebra.

- **CS 480: Senior Capstone Software Engineering Project (3 Credits)**
  - Description: Team-based software design, sprint tracking, and client deliverable implementation.
  - Prerequisites: CS 301, Senior Standing (minimum 90 earned credit hours), and departmental advisor approval.

## Degree Graduation Regulations
- Minimum Total Credit Hours: 120 credit hours.
- GPA Requirements: Cumulative overall GPA of at least 2.00, and major coursework GPA of at least 2.50 across all CS courses.
