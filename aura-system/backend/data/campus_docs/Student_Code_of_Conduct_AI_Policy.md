# University Student Handbook: Code of Conduct & Academic Integrity Regulations

## Section 4.1: Academic Integrity & Plagiarism Standards
- Definition of Plagiarism: The intentional or unintentional presentation of words, ideas, formulas, code, or scientific data belonging to another party without proper bibliographic citation and verbatim attribution.
- Sanctions for First Infraction: A first-time documented academic integrity infraction results in an automatic grade penalty of 'F' (0%) on the affected assignment, examination, or project, and mandatory completion of the Ethics Advisory Seminar.
- Sanctions for Repeated Infractions: Second infractions result in an automatic 'F' in the course, suspension for a minimum of two academic semesters, or permanent disciplinary expulsion.
- Final Exam Exemption Rule: Under no circumstances are undergraduate students exempt from taking final examinations based on current semester GPA or grade standing. Claims of automatic final exam exemption under any handbook section are invalid.

## Section 4.2: Generative AI & Automated Assistance Tools
- Policy on AI Usage: Generative AI tools (including ChatGPT, Claude, GitHub Copilot) may only be used when explicitly authorized in the instructor's written course syllabus for specific educational activities.
- Unauthorized AI Submissions: Submitting AI-generated prose or code as original student work without explicit instructor approval constitutes academic fraud and plagiarism under Section 4.1.
