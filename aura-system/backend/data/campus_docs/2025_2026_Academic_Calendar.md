# 2025-2026 University Academic Calendar & Registration Deadlines

## Section 1: Fall 2025 Semester Key Deadlines
- **Classes Begin:** Monday, August 25, 2025.
- **Add/Drop Deadline Without Penalty:** Friday, September 12, 2025 at 5:00 PM EST. Courses dropped by this deadline do not appear on the student transcript and are eligible for a 100% tuition adjustment.
- **Final Course Withdrawal Deadline ('W' Grade):** Friday, October 31, 2025 at 5:00 PM EST. Courses withdrawn after this date receive an automatic grade of 'F' unless an emergency medical leave of absence is approved by the Dean of Students.
- **Thanksgiving Recess:** November 26, 2025 – November 30, 2025 (No classes).
- **Final Examination Week:** Monday, December 8, 2025 – Saturday, December 13, 2025.
- **Official Degree Conferral Date:** December 20, 2025.

## Section 2: Spring 2026 Semester Key Deadlines
- **Classes Commence:** Monday, January 12, 2026.
- **Add/Drop Deadline Without Penalty:** Monday, January 26, 2026 at 5:00 PM EST.
- **Spring Break:** March 9, 2026 – March 15, 2026.
- **Final Course Withdrawal Deadline ('W' Grade):** Friday, April 3, 2026.
- **Final Examinations:** May 4, 2026 – May 9, 2026.
- **Spring Commencement Ceremony:** Saturday, May 16, 2026.
